# JS Streaming — Genre guide (custom RAG source)

Use this document during retrieval for listener intent, not for automated licensing decisions.

## Lofi & focus

- **Lofi** tracks are tagged for study, coding, and calm focus. Prefer **energy 0.30–0.50**, high **acousticness**, instrumental or soft vocals.
- Queries mentioning **focus**, **coding**, **library**, or **rain** should prioritize lofi and ambient rows before pop workout tracks.

## Pop & energy

- **Pop** + **happy** + high energy suits morning and workout contexts. Explicit rows may appear unless focus mode is on.

## Jazz & soul

- Jazz rows support **relaxed** and **after hours** moods; pair with low-to-mid energy unless the user asks for nightlife energy.

## Compliance note

Recommendations are **content-based only** on this demo catalog—never imply chart success or royalties.
